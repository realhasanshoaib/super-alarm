# Super Alarm Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-29

Super Alarm is an alarm app that helps make dismissal intentional. Android and iOS can recognize an enrolled household object or scan a QR code. On Android, alarms can open a full-screen ringing flow. On iOS, scheduled notifications open the dismissal flow when you tap them.

## Data stored on device

Super Alarm stores alarm settings, QR location labels and payloads, enrolled object labels and visual fingerprints, reliability checklist state, recent alarm attempts, and local diagnostic events on your device using local app storage. Android fingerprints are quantized model embeddings; iOS fingerprints are Apple Vision feature prints. The app does not store camera images or video.

## Camera

Super Alarm uses the camera to scan QR codes and to create and compare visual fingerprints for object-based alarm dismissal. Camera images and video are processed on device and are not uploaded, stored, or shared by Super Alarm.

## Notifications, alarms, and device permissions

Super Alarm uses notifications on iOS and Android. On Android, Super Alarm also uses exact alarms, full-screen alarm presentation, a foreground service, wake lock, vibration, boot restore, and audio settings so alarms can ring reliably at the scheduled time and after device restart.

## Diagnostics export

The app can export local diagnostics to a JSON file when you choose the export action. The export may include alarm settings, QR location labels and payloads, enrolled object labels and visual fingerprints, recent alarm attempts, reliability checklist state, and diagnostic events. This file stays under your control and is not sent to Super Alarm automatically. If you choose to send the export to support, support will receive the contents of that file for troubleshooting.

## Network, accounts, ads, and tracking

Super Alarm does not require an account, does not use ads, does not use third-party tracking, and does not send personal data to a Super Alarm server.

## Deleting data

You can delete app data by uninstalling Super Alarm or clearing the app storage from Android system settings. Android app backup is disabled for Super Alarm. Exported diagnostics files can be deleted from your device by you.
