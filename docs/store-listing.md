# Super Alarm store listing draft

## Title
Super Alarm — Scan to Wake Up

## Short description
Alarm clock for heavy sleepers. Get out of bed and scan an object to stop it.

## Google Play full description
You don't need a louder alarm. You need one you can't dismiss from under the covers.

Super Alarm only stops when you get out of bed and scan a real object in your home — your fridge, your mirror, your coffee machine — or a QR spot placed across the room. By the time you've dismissed it, you're already up.

Made for heavy sleepers and chronic snoozers
- Enroll any household object with a guided scan. No printer, no stickers.
- Or create a QR spot for the bathroom or kitchen.
- Walking across the room breaks the half-asleep snooze loop for good.

An alarm clock that actually rings
- Exact scheduled alarms that fire on time.
- Full-screen ringing even while your phone is locked.
- Backup re-rings if a ring is missed, and alarms restored after reboot.
- An optional wake-up test after setup to check ringing and dismissal on your phone.

Set up with confidence in minutes
- Guided setup for your dismissal target, alarm time, and repeat days.
- Alarm-critical phone settings checked in context, before they can silence you.
- Home-screen widget with your next alarm and 5h, 6h, and 8h sleep shortcuts.

Private by design
- Object recognition runs entirely on your phone.
- No account, no ads, no cloud sync. Alarm and scan features work offline.
- Camera frames are processed on device for QR scanning and object recognition. One representative object thumbnail stays in private app storage; every other frame is discarded.
- Optional anonymous product analytics can be turned off in the app. Essential crash diagnostics never include camera frames, QR payloads, or object fingerprints.
- Local diagnostics export when you want to verify reliability yourself.

Wake up on time. Get out of bed. Start the morning on your feet.

## Apple App Store description

Build a morning routine with a scan-to-finish wake-up challenge.

On iPhone and iPad, Super Alarm sends a scheduled notification. Tap it to start the in-app alarm sound, then scan an enrolled object or a QR spot across the room to finish the challenge.

Know how it works before relying on it:

- You must tap the notification to start continuous in-app sound.
- This version uses standard notifications, not Critical Alerts. Silent mode, Focus, and notification settings can silence or hide the reminder.
- It does not replace the iPhone Clock alarm. Keep a separate alarm if you need a wake-up sound without opening a notification.

Make getting up part of your routine:

- Enroll an object with a short guided scan, or create a QR spot for the bathroom or kitchen.
- Choose a time and repeat days, then allow notifications.
- Finish setup later without losing your progress. The home screen shows what remains.
- Try the optional wake-up test to check the notification and scan flow on your phone.

Private by design:

- Object recognition and QR scanning run on your iPhone.
- No account, no ads, no cloud sync. Alarm and scan features work offline.
- Camera frames, QR payloads, and object fingerprints are never sent to analytics or diagnostics providers.
- Optional anonymous product analytics can be turned off in Settings. Essential crash diagnostics help us find alarm reliability failures.
- Export local diagnostics when you need to check what happened.
