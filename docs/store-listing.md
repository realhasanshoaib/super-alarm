# Super Alarm store listing draft

## Title
Super Alarm

## Short description
A calm QR alarm that gets you out of bed.

## Google Play full description
Super Alarm turns waking up into a simple, intentional routine. Set your alarm, choose a real-world QR code location, and scan it when the alarm opens the QR dismissal flow.

Built with a calmer morning feel, Super Alarm keeps the flow focused: warm visuals, clear setup, and a reliable QR dismissal path for people who need more than a swipe-to-snooze alarm.

Platform note: Android supports a full-screen ringing flow for scheduled alarms. On iOS, scheduled notifications open the QR dismissal flow when tapped.

Features:
- QR-code dismissal flow
- Exact scheduled wake alarms on Android
- Full-screen ringing flow on Android
- Local notification wake flow on iOS
- Backup re-rings
- Boot restore on Android
- Reliability checklist
- Local diagnostics export

Super Alarm does not require an account and does not use ads or tracking.

## Google testing instructions
No account required. Create a QR location, grant alarm/notification/full-screen/camera permissions when prompted, then use Test Alarm to verify the alarm and QR dismissal flow.

## Apple review notes
No account required. Create a QR location, allow notifications and camera, enable the alarm, then use Test Alarm. On iOS, alarm delivery uses local notifications; tap the notification to open the QR dismissal flow.
